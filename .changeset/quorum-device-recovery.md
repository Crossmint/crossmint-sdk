Device recovery goes through a recovery quorum. With a member of a recovery quorum selected through `useRecoveryMethod`, `recover()` (and the automatic recovery before `send`, `approve` and the other calls) registers the new device signer with the member's quorum as approver. The registration is created unsigned, as with `prepareOnly`, and `recover()` returns it as `{ transactionId }` or `{ signatureId }` for the members to sign with `approve`. Until it lands, the device signer stays in `needs-recovery`, a later `recover()` returns the same request without registering or signing anything, the device key is kept, and the old device signer stays. The `recover()` that sees the registration land activates the device and starts the old device signer's removal through the quorum, returning that request the same way (automatic recovery logs it). At the signer limit, a quorum-approved recovery throws `SignerLimitExceededError` without removing a device. When the device signer's registration cannot be read and a quorum would approve, recovery stops with `WalletNotAvailableError` (try again later) instead of registering again; flat wallets go on as before. `recover()` now returns `RecoverResult` (it returned nothing before). A wallet whose recovery methods are all quorums now throws `RecoveryMethodRequiredError` until a member is selected, instead of `QuorumSignerNotSupportedError`.

A creating call (`send`, `sendTransaction`, `signMessage`, `signTypedData`, Stellar `upgrade` and `migrate`) on a device whose registration the quorum's members have not approved yet throws `DeviceSignerAwaitingApprovalError`, which carries the request they approve, before anything is created. Before, the call went on with the pending device signer and surfaced the API's rejection.
